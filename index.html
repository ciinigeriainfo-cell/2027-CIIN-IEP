Programme Overview

As Africa’s digital economy continues to expand, organisations are increasingly exposed to cyber risks arising from digital payments, cloud technology, artificial intelligence, data-driven businesses, and interconnected systems. This programme will examine how cyber insurance can support organisations in managing, mitigating, and transferring these emerging risks.

Hosted in Kigali, Rwanda, a city recognised for its growing digital economy, innovation ecosystem, and regional relevance, the programme provides an opportunity to examine cyber risk from an African perspective. Participants will explore emerging cyber threats, cyber insurance coverage, underwriting, risk assessment, claims management, incident response, and the challenges of developing effective cyber insurance solutions in African markets.

The programme will also highlight opportunities for insurers and other industry stakeholders to strengthen cyber resilience, develop innovative products, and support businesses navigating an increasingly digital environment.

Ultimately, the programme seeks to equip participants with practical knowledge of cyber risk management and insurance solutions while considering the evolving digital landscape and opportunities within Africa.

Programme Objectives

At the end of the programme, participants should be able to:

  • Understand the evolving cyber-risk landscape and its impact on businesses in Africa and globally.
  • Identify emerging cyber threats, including ransomware, data breaches, cyber fraud, AI-related risks, and third-party exposures.
  • Examine the role of cyber insurance in managing, mitigating, and transferring cyber risks.
  • Develop practical knowledge of cyber insurance underwriting, coverage, risk assessment, and claims management.
  • Explore opportunities and challenges in the growing African cyber insurance market.
  • Understand the importance of cybersecurity, resilience, and effective incident response in reducing cyber losses.
  • Leverage insights from Rwanda’s growing digital and technology ecosystem to appreciate emerging opportunities for insurers and businesses.
  • Enhance participants’ capacity to advise organisations on appropriate cyber risk management and insurance solutions.

Expected Participants

The programme is designed for:

  • • Chairmen, CEOs, Executive and Non-Executive Directors, Board Members, Company Secretaries and Board Advisers.
  • • Senior executives and professionals in insurance and reinsurance companies, brokers, loss adjusters, and regulatory and professional bodies.
